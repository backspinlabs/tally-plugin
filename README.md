# Tally

![Tally](assets/icon.png)

Tally answers questions about a shop's Lightspeed Retail (R-Series) data: sales, stock, service, customers, and the team. Ask in plain English, such as "How did we do yesterday?" or "Which jobs are waiting on parts?", and get the answer from a copy of the shop's data that refreshes every few minutes.

This plugin adds Tally's connector and six routines:

- **get-started**: what Tally sees for your shop, and a few first questions
- **morning-check**: yesterday, the month to date, and what needs attention today
- **month-so-far**: month-to-date sales and margin against last month and last year
- **stock-to-clear**: slow and aged stock, and what another store could sell
- **service-desk**: the work queue, jobs waiting on parts, and turnaround
- **end-of-day**: payments by type, and whether they tie to the day's sales

## What it connects to

The plugin connects to one remote server, `https://tally.backspinlabs.com/mcp`, run by Backspin Labs. You sign in with your Tally account the first time; your shop owner decides what you can see (costs, customers, and team figures). The skills are text only: they tell the assistant which Tally tools to call and how to lay out the answer. The plugin runs no code on your computer and sends nothing anywhere else.

Tally reads your shop's data. Changes in Lightspeed are off unless the shop owner turns them on, and each one is shown to you for approval before it is made.

## Requirements

A Lightspeed Retail (R-Series) shop connected to Tally. Sign up at [tally.backspinlabs.com](https://tally.backspinlabs.com).
